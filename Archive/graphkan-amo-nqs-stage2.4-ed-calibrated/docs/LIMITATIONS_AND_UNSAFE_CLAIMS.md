# Limitations and Unsafe Claims

## Main limitations

- The production VMC path is implemented as optional/import-safe infrastructure but was not run because NetKet, Flax and Optax were unavailable.
- QuSpin and TeNPy were unavailable, so ED and DMRG cross-checks are not claimed.
- The Rydberg chain pilot was CPU-reduced to N=8,10, two detunings and two seeds.
- The current models are exact-fullsum linear feature models, not the final trainable Flax/NetKet ansatz family.
- Graph-KAN was not run.
- Geometry transfer, robustness, PRA core, Bose-Hubbard, and IBM optional profiles were not run.
- No hardware result exists.
- No top-journal claim is supported.

## Safe claims

- The repository contains deterministic geometry and Rydberg Hamiltonian machinery.
- Dense/sparse and analytic-limit Rydberg tests pass.
- ED references and exact-fullsum pilot results are generated by scripts.
- The current CPU pilot does not show KAN-Jastrow superiority over matched MLP-pair or smooth Jastrow.
- Gauge-fixed learned-function exports exist and are seed-stable in the pilot, but final physical interpretability is unproven.
- Failed and not-run profiles are retained.

## Unsafe claims

- First KAN-NQS.
- First Graph-KAN.
- KANs are generally superior to MLPs/RBMs/RNNs/transformers.
- KAN-Jastrow is production-competitive on Rydberg arrays.
- Graph-KAN adds value.
- Hardware validation.
- Quantum advantage.
- Exact large-system physics.
- SOTA accuracy.
- Physical interpretability from one curve or from the present pilot alone.

## Kill conditions

- KAN-Jastrow remains worse than MLP-pair and smooth Jastrow under matched NetKet/Flax VMC conditions.
- Gauge-fixed functions become seed-dependent under stronger training.
- Graph-KAN is matched or beaten by graph MPNN with the same graph and features.
- Correlation errors remain poor even when energy is acceptable.
